# 🎯 Problem Statement

## The Core Crisis in College Programming Labs

In traditional university computer science and coding labs, one teaching assistant or professor oversees 40 to 70 students simultaneously during a 2-hour lab session.

### Key Pain Points:

1. **The TA Bottleneck**:
   - Students get stuck on minor syntax, off-by-one errors, or runtime bugs.
   - A student waits 15-20 minutes with their hand raised while the teacher is helping someone else.
   - Total lab productive time plummets by over 50%.

2. **The LLM Cheating Dilemma**:
   - When unassisted, students turn to ChatGPT, Copilot, or Claude.
   - These tools provide **direct copy-paste solutions**.
   - Students submit code they do not understand, resulting in zero long-term skill retention and rampant academic dishonesty.

3. **Faculty Blind Spots**:
   - Instructors have zero real-time visibility into who is currently struggling, what concept is causing widespread failures, or how many attempts a student made.
   - Grading only happens days later based on final code, missing the entire problem-solving journey.

4. **Code Similarity & Integrity**:
   - In a 60-person class, identical solutions circulate instantly. Faculty lack automated, live detection of structural plagiarism during the lab itself.

## CodeMentor AI's Solution
A white-label, real-time lab companion that provides **Socratic AI mentorship** directly inside the student's browser editor, while giving teachers a **live command center** showing student struggle metrics, error patterns, and real-time class progress.
