# 👥 Target Users & Personas

## Primary User Personas

### Persona 1: The Frustrated First-Year Student
- **Name:** Rohit Sharma (CS Freshman)
- **Context:** Enrolled in CS101: Introduction to Python Programming. Lab size: 65 students, 1 Teaching Assistant.
- **Pain Point:** Gets stuck on `IndexError: list index out of range` or off-by-one errors in loops. Raises hand and waits 20 minutes for TA assistance. When desperate, turns to ChatGPT, gets the direct code, copies it, and learns nothing.
- **Needs in CodeMentor AI:** Instant, gentle guidance that helps him realize his own bug without giving away the answer; an editor that auto-saves his work; a clear streak counter that builds coding confidence.

### Persona 2: The Overwhelmed Lab Instructor / Teaching Assistant
- **Name:** Dr. Priya Nair / TA Karthik
- **Context:** Manages 60+ students in a 2-hour practical coding lab.
- **Pain Point:** Constantly running between desks solving the exact same syntax misconception 30 times. Zero real-time insight into who is quietly struggling in the back of the room versus who has finished.
- **Needs in CodeMentor AI:** A real-time command dashboard showing a live grid of student status; automatic flagging of students with a "Struggle Score $\ge 3$"; aggregate analytics revealing class-wide conceptual roadblocks.

### Persona 3: College Academic Dean / Department Chair
- **Name:** Prof. M. K. Kulkarni (Head of Department)
- **Context:** Responsible for curriculum outcomes, accreditation standards (NBA/NAAC), and reducing student dropout rates in computer science.
- **Pain Point:** Rampant copy-paste plagiarism in student submissions; difficulty measuring true independent problem-solving skills.
- **Needs in CodeMentor AI:** Institutional white-label branding, transparent analytics on student learning curves, and an anti-cheating pedagogical structure.
